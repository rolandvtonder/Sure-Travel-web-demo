About us

Alberton's travel experts since 1969.

For more than five decades we've helped families, couples and businesses from the south of Johannesburg see the world, with the personal service of a local agency and the reach of a national travel group.

Meet the team Read reviews

(01) Our story

1969

A local name with global reach.

Sure Penzance Travel opened in 1969 and has been a trusted name in the Alberton community ever since.

Today we're part of the Sure Travel network, combining the personal service of a local agency with the buying power and global reach of one of South Africa's leading travel groups.

Our experienced consultants specialise in leisure, corporate, group and international travel, planning seamless, stress-free journeys around each client's needs, with strong global partnerships behind every booking.

(02) Then & now

Accredited and well connected.

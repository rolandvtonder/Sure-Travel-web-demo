# Sure Penzance Travel website (concept demo)

An unofficial redesign concept for [Sure Penzance Travel](https://www.suretravel.co.za/agency/sure-penzance-travel), a Sure Travel agency in Alberton, South Africa. This is not the agency's live website.

## Pages

- `index.html`: home
- `services.html`: travel services
- `corporate.html`: corporate and group travel
- `about.html`: story, team and reviews
- `contact.html`: trip planner and contact details

## Viewing it

It's a static site with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or upload the folder to any static host. An internet connection is needed for the web fonts and the animation libraries (GSAP and Lenis, loaded from jsDelivr).

## Notes

- The enquiry forms don't post to a server. They open WhatsApp or the visitor's email app with the enquiry filled in.
- Deal prices, travel dates and the Google rating were copied in September 2026 and will change.
- Office hours are marked "to be confirmed" on the contact page.
- Photos: destination images from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license); deal photos and the team portrait from suretravel.co.za. Icons: [Phosphor](https://phosphoricons.com) (MIT).

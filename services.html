(06) Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Call us on 011 907 9300 or send a WhatsApp message.

Do I have to come into the office?

No. Most trips are planned by phone, email or WhatsApp, but you're always welcome to visit us at 19 Penzance Street, Alberton.

Can you help with visas?

Yes. We'll guide you on the documents and visas your trip needs and point you to the right application process. You can also use our visa checker to see requirements for your destination.

Should I take travel insurance?

We always recommend it. Medical bills abroad can be very expensive, and good cover also protects you against cancellations, delays and lost luggage. Ask us to compare options for your trip.

Can I book online myself?

Yes. Our online tools for flights, hotels, car hire, transfers and activities are open around the clock. If you'd like a second opinion, send us your itinerary before you pay.

Do you book group trips?

Yes, from weddings and family reunions to sports tours and conferences. See Corporate & groups for how we handle them.

What if something goes wrong while I'm away?

Contact your consultant as soon as you can. We'll work with the airline, hotel or insurer to get things sorted, so you're not left to deal with it alone.

Not sure where to start?

Just ask us.

Tell us roughly where, when and who's travelling. We'll handle the rest.
